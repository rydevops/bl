How to play

Touch

  • Left thumb: drag to move
  • Right thumb: drag to look
  • Hold still on the right to mine
  • Tap on the right to place a block
  • Attack button: hold to mine or fight
  • Use button: place a block
  • ▲ jumps · hold it to swim up
  • Push the joystick past its ring to sprint

Keyboard & mouse

  • WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint
  • Mouse look · Left mine · Right place
  • 1-9 or wheel: hotbar
  • E inventory · Esc pause

Survive

  • Chop a tree: break its bottom log and the whole tree topples over
  • Pick up the drops, open the backpack
  • Craft planks, sticks, then a pickaxe and axe
  • Stone needs a pickaxe to drop cobblestone
  • Hoe the ground: craft a hoe (2 planks or cobblestone over 2 sticks) and Use it on dirt or grass. Cut tall grass for wheat seeds, plant them in the tilled soil and wait: soil with water within 4 blocks turns dark and grows wheat fastest. Harvest ripe (golden) wheat, craft 3 wheat into bread and eat it
  • A hammer (wood or stone, 5 planks or cobblestone + 2 sticks) breaks a 3x3 square of blocks at once, but slowly
  • Break leaves for apples, sticks and saplings; plant a sapling in dirt and it grows into a tree while you are nearby
  • Maths: to mine a new kind of block, answer a sum with the number pad. Right: mine as many of that block as you like. Wrong: it becomes TNT, so run! Cancel keeps things as they were
  • Eat apples with Use. Hunger drops as you work; sprinting needs 3 full drumsticks. Starving hurts, and if you die you drop everything
